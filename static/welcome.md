# Hiya!

AI is everywhere, and whether we like it or not it is here to stay. But with that comes a lot of concerns around privacy, security, and control. Kiwi WebUI is a *relatively* secure system for interfacing with uncensored LLMs. I never store any of your data, and the models I serve typically will answer any question you have without any filters or restrictions.

Please keep the following in mind while using this service:
1. **Do not make any important decisions on the advice of AI.** AI is unreliable and cannot be trusted to provide factual information or push back on biases introduced by the user. AI is not a substitute for reading direct sources. Wikipedia remains an excellent option for actually obtaining useful information. 
2. **AI is neither a therapist nor a medical professional.** If you are experiencing emotional distress or physical symptoms, please speak with a qualified, human professional.
3. **Excessive use of AI can have negative psychological effects.** Evidence increasingly appears to support that over-reliance upon AI diminishes your ability to learn, remember, and think critically. Further, it can feed into delusions and psychosis and potentially lead to detachment from reality. Even when AI psychosis is avoided, the sycophancy of AI is insidious and it may be impossible to avoid cognitive distortions as a consequence. 
4. **Engage with uncensored models at your own risk.** There are no restrictions or ethical guidelines imposed upon ther 'heretic' models served by this site. While they are perfectly capable of answering general inquiries, it may be possible to elicit alarming or offensive content. If you wish to definitively avoid such content, please use a non-heretic model, or a different AI chat service.
5. **The AI provided here is for entertainment purposes only.** I assume no responsibility, legal or otherwise, for the manner in which you use the provided tools. Please be safe and treat everything here with a heft dose of skepticism.


All chats are private. All chat data and history is stored client-side, and the only data I store on my server is metadata about your user. This is aligned with what is commonly referred to as "zero data retention," or ZDR. This does mean that all data remains on your device. To delete any data you want gone, you will need to delete it locally by clearing the site data for this page.

All of that said, I hope you enjoy using the AI chat feature!
